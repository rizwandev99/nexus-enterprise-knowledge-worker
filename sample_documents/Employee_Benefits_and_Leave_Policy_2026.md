# Global Tech Inc. - Employee Handbook & Benefits Guide 2026

## 1. Welcome to Global Tech Inc.!
We believe in a happy, healthy, and productive team. Our work culture is built on trust and flexibility:
- **Work Week**: Monday to Friday (5-day work week). Saturday & Sunday are completely off.
- **Flexible Timings**: No strict punch-in times. As long as you complete your daily work and attend scheduled team meetings, you choose your hours.

## 3. Health, Wellness & Medical Insurance

Your health and your family's health are our top priority:

1. **Family Medical Insurance**: 
   - 100% company-sponsored health insurance covering up to **₹10,00,000 per year**.
   - Covers employee, spouse, and up to 2 children + parents.
   - Cashless hospitalization in over 8,000 network hospitals.
2. **Gym & Fitness Allowance**: 
   - Reimbursement of up to **₹3,000 per month** for gym memberships, yoga classes, or sports club fees.
3. **Free Annual Health Checkup**: 
   - One comprehensive free health checkup package every year for you and your spouse.

---

## 4. Remote Work & Home Office Setup Perks

Whether you work from home or anywhere in the world, we support your comfort:

- **One-Time Home Office Setup**: Every new employee gets a **₹50,000 (or $600 USD)** one-time allowance to buy an ergonomic chair, standing desk, or external monitor.
- **Monthly High-Speed Internet**: Get up to **₹2,000 per month** reimbursed for your home WiFi and broadband connection.
- **Laptop & Equipment**: Apple MacBook Pro or high-end Dell XPS laptop provided by the company, replaced every 3 years.

---

## 5. Food, Travel & Annual Company Retreat

- **Daily Lunch Allowance**: ₹400 per working day provided as food card / Swiggy-Zomato vouchers.
- **Annual Company Retreat (Goa / Bali)**: 
  - Every year in November, the entire global team meets for a 4-day **all-expenses-paid trip** (Flights, 5-star hotel stay, food, and fun team activities are 100% paid by the company).

---

## 6. How to Claim Reimbursements (3 Simple Steps)

1. Keep your bill or payment receipt (PDF or photo).
2. Login to the HR Portal at `portal.globaltech.com`.
3. Click **"Submit Expense"**, select the category (Gym / WiFi / Setup), and attach your receipt.
4. *All approved expenses are credited directly to your bank account with your monthly salary on the 1st of every month.*
